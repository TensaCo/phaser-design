# v2 — what changed and why

v2 is the redesign requested after v1: a product film with founder energy, closer to a premium hardware launch.
Same product, same simulator, same disclosures. Running time 3:32 (3:28 of score, then a 4-second disclosure card).

| | v1 | v2 |
|---|---|---|
| Voices | anonymous narrator, unnamed engineer, two customers | **Mei-Lin Zhou** (Chief Scientist, PHASER) and **Diane Kowalski** (CEO), TensaCo's AI-agent personas (tensaco.ai/company/leadership), plus one dramatized customer. No narrator. |
| Interviews | garage and office documentary | **white-studio sit-down interviews** (Mei-Lin to camera at the open and the climax), with the garage kept as the "where it was built" documentary |
| Product | darkroom renders | darkroom + **white-studio** renders, and an **exploded view** (assembling in the dark; coming apart in the studio with leader-line labels) |
| Score | synthesized pad | **composed score** in seven sections, the edit cut to it: near-silent cold open → hero theme → intimate piano middle → rising build → strings for the human stakes → hero theme returns → motif alone and fade (`gen/v2/music/score-1.mp3`, ElevenLabs Music) |
| Photography | grade + grain | + depth of field from the render's depth pass, handheld micro-motion, tripod "breath" on interviews, lens chromatic aberration, halation, exposure breathing, rack focus; all seeded per frame |
| Customer energy line | a customer says "that's our energy bill?" | Diane tells it as a story ("she asked if that was really the energy number"), on screen: "Dramatization. PHASER energy figures are modeled." |

## Disclosures (unchanged in kind)
- `PLANNED FUTURE PRODUCT LAUNCH VIDEO` in the centre of every frame of every version.
- Every person has a lower third on first appearance; Mei-Lin and Diane: "AI agent persona · dramatization · AI-generated"; the customer: "dramatization · AI-generated". Every generated shot carries a top-right "DRAMATIZATION · AI-GENERATED PEOPLE, PLACES AND VOICES" / "AI-GENERATED IMAGERY" tag; stock is tagged "STOCK FOOTAGE".
- Every PHASER number is labelled modeled; the same numbers and sources as `CLAIMS.md`.
- The closing card states the planned-future framing, the dramatization, that Mei-Lin and Diane are AI-agent personas, that figures are modeled, and credits the score and footage.
